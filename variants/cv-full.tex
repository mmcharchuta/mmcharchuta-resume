\section{Profile}
{\small
\textbf{Bioinformatician turning exploratory genomics into reproducible, production-grade pipelines.}
I build containerised, re-runnable analyses (Nextflow, Snakemake, Docker, Conda) and specialise in chromosome conformation capture (Meta Hi-C, 3C-seq) and machine learning on bacterial pangenomes.
At Phagos I turn Meta Hi-C data into phage--host pairings for therapeutic discovery.
At Adam Mickiewicz University (AMU) I run an independently grant-funded project on the 3D genome of \textit{Bacillus thuringiensis} and mine its pangenome for Cry protein toxins.
Earlier I worked in personalised medicine, cellular agriculture and commercial biosafety analytics, so I am used to QA standards, turnaround times and teams that mix wet lab and code.
Three publications and invited teaching on reproducibility; EU remote-first, open to B2B contracts and industrial PhD (CIFRE) partnerships.\par}
